# Boss Design — Manananggal (Act 1)
Environment = skyway arena with verticality (ramps, rooftops, rain).
Mechanic list (master doc §45): airborne attack, landing attack, scream (audio locate), separation (phase 2 torso), swarm summon, rooftop movement, vulnerable phase, extraction countdown.
HUD: segmented boss bar top-center (both renderers). Audio: wing flap + shriek panned by bearing (FP listener).
Counterplay: vulnerable when landed/separated; force players to track vertically (3D camera pitch comes with milestone 4+).
